# Evidence checklist

Capture screenshots or sanitized records showing:

1. Built-in policy settings and assignment.
2. Custom compliance discovery script uploaded to Intune.
3. Custom compliance JSON accepted by the policy wizard.
4. Lab policy assignment to the test group.
5. Device-level compliance status.
6. Per-setting CompanyPortalInstalled result.
7. Local discovery-script output.
8. Remediation and retest outcome.
9. Cleanup or rollback verification.

Redact tenant IDs, user information, serial numbers, and other sensitive
details when they are not required to demonstrate the result.
